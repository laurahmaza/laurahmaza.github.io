Research Question

Do large language models’ next-token probability distributions shift when semantic content is held constant but surface linguistic form varies — voice, clause subordination, register?

This work measures that shift directly, using distributional divergence between next-token predictions rather than surface-level text comparison, to identify which linguistic dimensions a model treats as meaningful versus superficial.

Why this question? A decade spent reading, teaching, and analyzing English language and literature means fluency in exactly the categories this question depends on: voice, subordination, register, and the difference between what a sentence says and how it says it. That background is the methodological advantage here, not a detour from it.
