Laura H. Maza

Software engineer working in risk and AI governance for highly regulated industries · Independent AI Researcher

I work where AI systems meet accountability. For more than twenty years I have moved between language, software and compliance: as a professor of English language and literature, a software engineer and CTO, and a governance and compliance lead in regulated industries.

Today that becomes two lines of work that feed each other. In practice, I help organizations govern risk and adopt AI under frameworks like ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI RMF. In research, I measure how large language models react when the wording of a prompt changes but its meaning does not.
